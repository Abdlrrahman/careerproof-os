# Content & Evidence Guide — CareerProof OS

## 1. Content Philosophy & Editorial Voice
- **Tone**: Technically credible, direct, outcome-focused, globally employable, human, and transparent.
- **Rule of Evidence**: Every single claim, metric, and proficiency level must be backed by a verifiable record.
- **Prohibited Terminology**: Never use hollow buzzwords ("ninja", "rockstar", "guru", "visionary evangelist", "coding wizard").
- **Honest Gap Disclosure**: If a skill or experience is not supported by direct production implementation, disclose it as `familiar` or `self-reported` rather than exaggerating.

---

## 2. Evidence Classification Hierarchy

| Level | Definition | Required Evidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Familiar** | Learned through coursework or limited experimentation | 1 course, tutorial, or minor script |
| **Applied** | Implemented in at least one real project or feature | 1+ production or verified project |
| **Proven** | Multiple production deployments with measured outcomes | 2+ projects with quantifiable metrics |
| **Advanced** | Core deep expertise across large or complex systems | 4+ enterprise implementations |
| **Leadership** | Directed architecture, team delivery, or organizational standards | Lead/Manager role with team scope |

---

## 3. Verification Badging Guidelines

* **Verified**: Backed by public code repository, active production URL, university transcript, accredited certificate credential ID, or institutional reference.
* **Verification Required**: Self-reported outcome or ongoing deliverable awaiting third-party sign-off.
* **Concept / In Development**: Architectural specification, prototype, or research initiative clearly distinguished from completed production systems.
